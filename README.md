# Coffee Shop Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Summary
This project focuses on analyzing retail coffee shop sales data to uncover trends in customer behavior, sales performance, and product demand. An interactive dashboard was developed to support data-driven decision-making and business insights.

## Business Objective
To analyze historical sales data and identify:
- Peak sales hours and days
- Monthly revenue trends
- Store-wise performance
- Product and category-level sales insights
- Customer spending behavior

## Dataset Overview
The dataset consists of transactional sales records containing:
- Order date and time
- Store location
- Product category and product type
- Quantity sold
- Sales revenue

## Analysis Performed
- Time-based analysis (hourly, daily, monthly trends)
- Sales performance comparison across store locations
- Product-level and category-level sales analysis
- Identification of top-selling products by revenue and quantity
- Average order value and customer purchasing patterns

## Dashboard Features
- KPI metrics: Total Sales, Total Footfall, Average Bill per Person
- Hourly and weekday sales trend analysis
- Store location performance comparison
- Product category and size distribution
- Interactive filters for improved analysis

## Key Insights
- Sales peak during morning hours (7 AM – 10 AM), indicating strong breakfast demand
- Coffee is the highest-revenue product category
- Monthly sales show a consistent upward trend from January to June
- Hell’s Kitchen generates the highest revenue, while Astoria has the highest footfall
